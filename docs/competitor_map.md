# Competitor Map (v1, Oct 24 2026)

Verify pricing on interview calls: most vendors don't publish prices, so the figures below are third-party estimates.

| Player | Category | What they do | Price signal | Our angle |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fleetx | Fleet mgmt (India) | Tracking, fuel, maintenance, predictive maintenance module | Custom quote. Third-party estimate $20–60/vehicle/month software + $100–600 hardware ([costbench](https://costbench.com/software/fleet-management/fleetx/)) | Too heavy for a 15-vehicle operator; we plug into existing data |
| Intangles | Predictive vehicle health (India) | Digital-twin style health + breakdown prediction for fleets/OEMs. Raised $30M ([DealStreetAsia](https://media.dealstreetasia.com/stories/intangles-avataar-venture-partners-459079)) | Not public | Closest competitor. We differentiate on small-fleet price, explainability, no new hardware |
| Samsara / Motive / Geotab | Global telematics | Full telematics suites, maintenance workflows, predictive insights | Enterprise contracts | Not targeting small Indian fleets |
| Pitstop | Predictive maintenance | Fleet uptime, maintenance prioritization | Enterprise | Same idea, different market |
| GaragePro / AutoHealth | OBD diagnostics (India) | Scanner, fault codes, health reports | One-time / low cost | They show raw data; we give a decision |
| Hyundai Bluelink etc. | OEM connected car | Health + service alerts for owners | Bundled with car | Only new cars and single owners. Skip consumers |
| Basic GPS trackers (AIS-140 vendors) | Tracking | Location, overspeed, ignition | ~₹100–300/vehicle/month (verify) | **Our wedge:** add decisions on top of what they already have |

## Positioning
"We don't replace your tracker. We make the vehicle data you already have tell you which vehicle needs attention today, and why."

## Open questions for interviews
- What do small fleets actually pay for their basic trackers today?
- Do any of them already get predictive alerts, and do they trust them?
- Fleetx/Intangles minimum fleet size and price for a 10–30 vehicle operator (call their sales as a "prospect").
